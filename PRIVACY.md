# Privacy Policy for Alex vs Buffy

**Effective date: 16 October 2026**

Alex vs Buffy ("the app") is a short story game for Android and iPhone. It is made by Merlin Robin Stemmer ("I", "me", the developer). This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Short version

The app collects no data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not connect to the internet.

## What the app does

You read a short story and pick what happens next. Your choices are only used to show the next page. They are not saved and are not sent anywhere.

## What the app does not do

The app:

- Has no account or sign-in
- Shows no ads and does not use advertising identifiers
- Does not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data
- Does not include analytics or crash reporting tools
- Does not track you across other apps or websites
- Does not sell or share any data

## Retention and deletion

The app keeps no personal data, on your device or anywhere else, so there is nothing to delete. Uninstalling the app removes it completely.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It does not collect personal information from anyone.
